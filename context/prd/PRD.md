# EXTROVELA — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision & Mission
- **Name**: EXTROVELA
- **Tagline**: Stop scrolling. Start experiencing. Don't just get through your day. Make it different.
- **Mission**: A personalized real-world experience app designed to help users break out of repetitive routines, leave their rooms, discover new places, and build life stories to tell.
- **Core Philosophy**: Life meaning comes from real-world experiences. EXTROVELA is anti-productivity gamification — no toxic streaks or grinding points.

## 2. Core User Persona & Pain Point
- **Persona**: Individuals who enjoy solo time or have few friends by choice, but occasionally feel lonely or bored. They want to leave their room and go outside, but experience decision paralysis ("I want to go out, but I don't know what to do").
- **Solution**: A daily quest generator that offers 3 personalized real-world quest options tailored to available time, energy, mood, budget, season (Garimahina/Summer vs Jadamahina/Winter), and city location (Kathmandu, Pokhara, Global).

## 3. Product Loop
1. **Choose Parameters**: Time, Energy, Mood, Budget, Social Preference, Environment, Season, City.
2. **Receive 3 Quest Options**: Personal, contextual, achievable, unexpected.
3. **Experience**: Real-world execution (with optional phone-free mode).
4. **Capture**: Photo snapshot, mood rating, reflection note, voice note.
5. **Remember**: Calendar Journal grid & Open-World Fog-of-War Life Map.
6. **Discover More**: Routine-breaker analytics & monthly recaps ("Your August").

## 4. Key Target Features
- 3-Option Quest Generator Engine
- Phone-Free Focus Mode & Experience Timer
- Photo & Voice Reflection Memory Journal
- Open-World Video Game Fog of War Life Map
- Anti-Gamification Experience Metrics & Monthly Recaps
- Optional Co-Questing & Safe Friend Invite Links
