# EXTROVELA — Quest Generation Catalog & Rules

## Core Generator Rules
1. **Three Choices Rule**: Always generate 3 distinct options for the user to compare and choose one.
2. **Context Sensitivity**:
   - City Specific (Kathmandu viewpoints/streets vs Pokhara lakeside/pagoda).
   - Season Specific (Garimahina/Summer swim vs Jadamahina/Winter fancy drink).
3. **Routine Breaker Logic**: Detects repetitive indoor solo picks and gently prompts outdoor/social stepping.

## Primary Quests Catalog Highlights
- **Sunset Viewpoint**: Watch sunset from Swayambhunath / Sarangkot viewpoint without phone.
- **Cloud Gazing**: 15-minute phone-free cloud watching.
- **Studio Ghibli Movie Night**: Phone-free cozy movie session.
- **Reconnect Friend**: Call an old friend without any hidden agenda.
- **Compliments to Strangers**: Give genuine compliments to 5 strangers.
- **Solo Café Reader**: Read a physical book alone in a local teahouse.
- **Mystery Bus Ride**: Ride public bus to final stop and explore.
- **Garimahina Swim**: Summer lake or pool swim.
- **Jadamahina Elixir**: Craft a winter spiced hot drink from scratch.
