# 01. EXTROVELA — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Core Vision & Mission
- **Name**: EXTROVELA
- **Tagline**: *Stop scrolling. Start experiencing. Don't just get through your day. Make it different.*
- **Mission**: EXTROVELA is a personal real-world experience engine designed to break people out of repetitive routines, discover new places, try new things, and build stories to tell.
- **Anti-Productivity Philosophy**: Unlike traditional productivity apps, EXTROVELA measures real-world stories and first-time experiences rather than to-do list items, points, or addictive daily streaks.

## 2. Core Problem & Target Users
- **Target User**: Individuals who get lonely or bored, have few friends by choice, enjoy spending time alone, but lack real-world experiences. They want to leave their room and go outside, but suffer from decision paralysis ("I want to go out, but I don't know what to do").
- **Core Problem**: Daily routine trap, endless screen scrolling, and lack of contextual guidance for spontaneous real-life exploration.

## 3. Fundamental Product Loop
```
Choose Parameters (Time, Energy, Mood, Budget, Social, Environment, City, Season)
       ↓
Receive 3 Tailored Quest Options (Kathmandu / Pokhara / Global Context)
       ↓
Start Real-World Experience (Optional Phone-Free Sanctuary Mode)
       ↓
Capture Snapshot & Reflection (Photo, Audio Voice Note, Mood Scale)
       ↓
Save to Calendar Journal & Reveal Open-World Fog-of-War Life Map
       ↓
Discover More & Break Routine Patterns
```

## 4. Key Product Requirements
- **Daily Quest Flow**: Present 3 distinct personalized quest cards every time the user requests options.
- **Quest Completion**: Phone-free countdown timer, step guidance, real-time sunset detector.
- **Memory Engine**: Photo snapshot, 1-5 star feeling scale, reflection note, 15-second audio snippet, geotag, and first-time milestone tag.
- **Calendar Journal**: Visual grid of photo memories by completion date.
- **Life Map**: Open-world video game Fog-of-War map that unlocks as quests are logged.
- **Social Features**: One-click friend invite link generator & safe public quester matcher.
- **Recaps**: "Your August" experience summaries measuring new places, first-time experiences, and routine breaks.
