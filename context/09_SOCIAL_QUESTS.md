# 09. EXTROVELA — Social Quests & Friend Sharing

## 1. Shareable Quest Links
- Users can generate a direct share link (`https://extrovela.app/quest/:id?invite=true`) to send to a friend via SMS, WhatsApp, or Instagram.
- The recipient opens the link and sees the matching quest card with options to join or try the same experience independently.

## 2. Public Co-Quest Matching
- Optional toggle for users looking for a low-pressure companion in the same city for public outdoor quests (e.g. sunset viewing, cafe reading, street walk).
