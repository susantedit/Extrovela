# 08. EXTROVELA — Auth, Privacy & Safety Specification

## 1. Privacy Principles
- **Private by Default**: Personal reflections, photos, and exact location coordinates remain strictly private on the user's device unless explicitly shared via link.
- **Geotag Privacy**: Exact home address coordinates are never stored or exposed; only general district / landmark pins are placed on the Life Map.

## 2. Public Quest & Stranger Co-Questing Safety Protocols
- **Low-Pressure Greetings**: No open direct messaging with strangers before mutual opt-in approval.
- **Public Meeting Safety Flags**: Meeting suggestions strictly recommend public open daylight spaces (cafés, parks, viewpoints).
- **Report & Block Controls**: One-click reporting and blocking for public quester matching.
