# 03. EXTROVELA — UX & Screen Architecture Specification

## 1. Complete Experience User Flow
```
Onboarding (Personality & Home City Setup)
       ↓
Home Dashboard (Discover Quests)
       ↓
Choose Today's Conditions (Time, Energy, Mood, Budget, Social, Environment)
       ↓
AI Quest Generator Output (3 Tailored Quest Options)
       ↓
Quest Detail & Acceptance
       ↓
Start Experience (Phone-Free Mode & Timer)
       ↓
Experience Completion & Reflection Capture
       ↓
Memory Logged to Calendar Journal & Open-World Life Map
       ↓
Monthly Recaps ("Your August")
```

## 2. Screen Definitions & States
1. **Discover Quests Screen**: Filter matrix controls + 3-option quest choices display.
2. **Active Quest Screen**: Live experience timer, phone-free focus banner, step guidance, golden hour status, and share invite link.
3. **Capture Memory Modal**: Photo snapshot upload/preset picker, 1-5 star mood rating, reflection text area, 15-second audio reflection, location input, and first-time tag.
4. **Calendar Journal Screen**: Month memory grid, photo story cards, tag filters (First-Time, Sunsets, Cafés).
5. **Life Map Screen**: Leaflet interactive canvas, undiscovered fog of war, memory pins, and city unlocked percentage.
6. **Recaps View Screen**: "Your August" story metrics card, routine breaker distribution chart, and shareable card button.
