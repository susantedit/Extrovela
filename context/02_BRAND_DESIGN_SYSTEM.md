# 02. EXTROVELA — Brand & Design System Specifications

## 1. Official Brand Logo
- **Logo Asset**: [`public/logo.png`](file:///f:/Extrovela/public/logo.png) / [`EXTROVELA — Brand Logo.png`](file:///f:/Extrovela/EXTROVELA%20—%20Brand%20Logo.png)
- **Visual Emblem**: Sharp geometric open-cube container with an upward-pointing vibrant lime green arrow breaking out (`#84CC16` / `#A3E635`), symbolizing stepping out of your routine into real-life adventure.
- **Brand Typography**: High-contrast geometric typography for **EXTROVELA** with a signature sharp diagonal slash through the "X".

## 2. Typography Scale
- **Display Headlines**: `Outfit` (Weight 700 / 800 / 900, Letter-Spacing `-0.03em`)
- **Body Text & Controls**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weight 400 / 500 / 600, Letter-Spacing `-0.015em`)
- **Monospace Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono` for timers, percentages, and coordinates.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Background**: Deep Dark Ambient (`#08090D` / `#0A0B0E`)
- **Brand Lime Accent**: Dynamic Arrow Lime Green (`#84CC16` / `#A3E635`)
- **Card Background**: Glassmorphism (`rgba(22, 26, 38, 0.75)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`)
- **Gradients**:
  * Brand Sunset & Lime Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #84CC16 0%, #F59E0B 50%, #F43F5E 100%)`)
  * Neon Violet Glow (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`)
- **Text**: Primary White (`#F9FAFB`), Muted Gray (`#9CA3AF`)

## 4. UI Components & Micro-Interactions
- **Glass Buttons**: Interactive hover glow with scale transformations.
- **Pulsing Rings**: Phone-free sanctuary pulsing animation.
- **Confetti Celebrations**: `canvas-confetti` explosion on memory capture.
